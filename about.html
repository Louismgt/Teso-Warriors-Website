WHO WE ARE

Building Something That Lasts.

Teso Warriors Football Club is a football institution based in the Teso Sub-region of Uganda. The club is committed to developing talented footballers, responsible leaders and a sustainable football structure.

Our ambition extends beyond results on the pitch. We are building an environment where players can develop their abilities, understand responsibility and represent their community with pride.

Through structured football, strong leadership and continuous improvement, Teso Warriors FC is building a club designed for long-term growth.
