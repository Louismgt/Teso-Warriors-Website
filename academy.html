TESO WARRIORS ACADEMY

Developing The Future.

The Teso Warriors FC Academy is an important part of the club's long-term football development structure.

Its purpose is to identify talented young footballers and provide an environment where they can develop their technical, tactical, physical and personal abilities.

The academy is built around the belief that football development should prepare young people not only to compete, but also to become responsible leaders.

OUR APPROACH

More Than Football.

The Warrior Pathway combines football development with discipline, responsibility, teamwork and continuous improvement.

01

Technical Development

Building strong fundamental football skills through structured training and repetition.

02

Tactical Understanding

Helping players understand the game, make better decisions and develop their football intelligence.

03

Physical Development

Supporting age-appropriate physical development, movement, fitness and athletic preparation.

04

Character Development

Developing discipline, respect, responsibility, teamwork and leadership alongside football ability.

THE PATHWAY

Identify. Develop. Compete. Progress.

01

Identify

Discover players with potential through scouting, football activities and community engagement.

02

Develop

Provide structured training and guidance to help players improve their overall football ability.

03

Compete

Give players opportunities to test their development through competitive football.

04

Progress

Support players as they progress within the club or toward higher levels of football.

WARRIOR STANDARD

What We Expect.

02
