TESO WARRIORS ACADEMY

Talent Is Only The Beginning.

The Teso Warriors Academy is a long-term player development pathway designed to identify, develop and prepare young footballers for higher levels of the game.

The academy is built around football development, discipline, education, character and responsibility. Players are expected to grow both as footballers and as people.

The ultimate objective is to create a clear pathway from youth football into senior competition while contributing to the wider development of football in the Teso Sub-region.

OUR APPROACH

Develop The Whole Player.

01

Technical

Developing ball mastery, passing, receiving, finishing, defending and the technical foundations required for the game.

02

Tactical

Helping players understand the game, make better decisions and develop their tactical awareness through structured training and competition.

03

Physical

Building age-appropriate physical qualities while promoting healthy habits, conditioning and injury prevention.

04

Mental

Developing confidence, resilience, concentration, competitiveness and the ability to respond positively to challenges.

05

Character

Teaching discipline, respect, responsibility, teamwork and the standards expected of a Teso Warriors player.

06

Leadership

Creating opportunities for young players to develop leadership qualities and become positive influences within their teams and communities.

WARRIOR STANDARDS

What We Expect.

01

Discipline

Respect training, teammates, coaches, opponents and the rules of the game.

02

Commitment

Approach development with consistency, effort and a willingness to improve.

03

Respect

Represent yourself, your family, your team and Teso Warriors FC with dignity.

04

Responsibility

Understand that being a footballer also means developing good character and taking responsibility for your actions.

THE ULTIMATE PATHWAY

From Academy
To Senior Football.

The academy is an important part of the club's long-term football structure. The objective is to create a genuine pathway through which players can develop and, when ready, progress into senior football.

Progression will be based on development, performance, discipline, readiness and the needs of the player and team.
