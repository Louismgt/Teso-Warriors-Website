Teso Warriors FC Academy Coming Soon

The Next Generation Is Coming.

Teso Warriors FC is developing a future pathway for young football talent across the Teso Sub-region. The Academy is currently under development and will be launched when the club is ready to provide the right structure, environment and opportunities for young players.

Building for the Future

A Pathway With Purpose.

The Teso Warriors FC Academy is not yet operational. We are currently developing the structure, systems and football pathway required to build a sustainable youth development programme.

When launched, the Academy will support the club's long-term ambition of discovering talented young footballers, nurturing their development and creating opportunities for progression within and beyond Teso Warriors FC.

The Warrior Pathway

Discover. Nurture. Promote. Progress.

Our future development pathway is built around four simple principles that reflect how Teso Warriors FC intends to identify and support football talent.

01

Discover

Identify talented young footballers through grassroots football, scouting, community engagement and open opportunities.

02

Nurture

Provide the right training environment, guidance, discipline and support for player development.

03

Promote

Create opportunities for players to compete, demonstrate their ability and earn greater responsibility.

04

Progress

Help players advance within Teso Warriors FC or move towards higher levels and better football opportunities.

Important Notice

Registration Is Not Yet Open.

Teso Warriors FC is currently not accepting Academy registrations, applications or trial requests. Official information regarding the Academy launch, age groups, recruitment, registration and participation will be communicated through the club's official channels when the programme is ready.
