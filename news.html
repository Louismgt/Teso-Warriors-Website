Warriors Begin 2026/27 Campaign at Home Against Rupa United

Teso Warriors FC will begin its 2026/27 NERFA Regional League campaign at home against Rupa United FC.

Competition NERFA Regional League
Date Friday, 30 October 2026
Kick-Off 3:00 PM
Venue Soroti Stadium
League Opener / 2026-27

Teso Warriors FC turns its attention to the opening challenge of the new season as the Warriors prepare to welcome Rupa United FC to Soroti Stadium.

The fixture marks the beginning of the club's first-round NERFA Regional League programme for the 2026/27 season.

The team enters the campaign focused on discipline, continuous development and competing strongly while proudly representing the Teso Sub-region.

Players and technical staff will now complete their final preparations ahead of Friday's 3:00 PM kick-off.

Supporters are encouraged to follow the club's official channels and website for matchday information, results and club updates throughout the season.
