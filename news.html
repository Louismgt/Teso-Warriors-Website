First Team / Match Report 7 November 2026

Teso Warriors Earn Deserved Away Win Over Ateker FC

Teso Warriors FC secured a hard-fought 1-0 away victory over Ateker FC in a physical North East Regional League contest at Moroto Booma Grounds.

Competition North East Regional League
Result Ateker FC 0-1 Teso Warriors FC
Date Saturday, 7 November 2026
Venue Moroto Booma Grounds, Moroto
Match Report / Away Victory

Teso Warriors FC secured an important 1-0 away victory over Ateker FC in the North East Regional League, continuing a strong start to the 2026/27 campaign.

The match at Moroto Booma Grounds was a highly physical contest, with both teams competing strongly throughout. The Warriors matched the intensity of the hosts, remained disciplined and stayed focused in difficult away conditions.

Despite the physical nature of the game, Teso Warriors produced the better overall performance and created enough chances to deserve all three points.

The team showed determination, organisation and attacking intent, repeatedly finding its way into dangerous areas and creating opportunities to extend the advantage.

While the victory was deserved, the performance also highlighted an important area for improvement. The Warriors created enough chances to make the result more comfortable and will continue working to improve their finishing.

Key Takeaways

Deserved victory: Teso Warriors created enough opportunities and did enough over the course of the match to merit the three points.

Physical contest: Ateker FC provided a demanding challenge, but the Warriors competed strongly and maintained their discipline.

Positive chance creation: The team continued to create opportunities in dangerous attacking areas.

Finishing remains a focus: Greater efficiency in front of goal could have made the result more comfortable, and this remains an area the team hopes to improve.

The victory takes Teso Warriors to two wins from their opening two league matches, with five goals scored and one conceded.
